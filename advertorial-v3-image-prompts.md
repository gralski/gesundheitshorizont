# Derma Renew Advertorial V3 - final image prompts

All four assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool in the `photorealistic-natural` category. Final WebP files are stored in `public/images/advertorial-v3/`.

## Hero portrait

Create a hyper-realistic, candid editorial portrait of a confident German woman around 60 at home, photographed in a quiet, unposed moment while looking at a family photo on her phone. Show authentic mature skin with pores, fine lines, slight under-eye texture and a natural expression. Use soft overcast window light, a pale blue blouse, a contemporary German interior and full-frame 50mm camera realism. Landscape 3:2, waist-up, woman slightly offset with calm negative space. No visible product, text, logo, watermark, beauty retouching, glamour makeup, exaggerated sadness, de-aging or before-after framing.

## Skincare drawer

Create a hyper-realistic editorial photograph of an open bathroom vanity drawer slightly overfilled with premium but unbranded creams, serums, eye care, masks and one forgotten collagen powder scoop. Use a pale stone vanity, clean contemporary German bathroom, cool morning daylight, exact glass and ceramic texture and a landscape 4:3 overhead three-quarter angle. The result should feel lived-in and relatable, not dirty or chaotic. No readable brands, text, logo, watermark, luxury-hotel sterility or symmetrical product grid.

## Two-capsule routine

Create a hyper-realistic morning bathroom photograph showing the hands of a woman around 60 holding exactly two dark charcoal supplement capsules beside a small glass of water and a simple unbranded moisturizer. Use age-appropriate hand texture, a cool pale stone counter, a softly blurred mirror and window, clean natural daylight and a landscape 3:2 close-up. Communicate a calm routine that takes less than a minute. No bottle, face, readable label, text, logo, watermark, medical setting, excessive pills, young hands or warped fingers.

## Closing mirror portrait

Create a hyper-realistic photograph of a woman around 62 looking at herself in a bathroom mirror after getting ready, relaxed and quietly pleased with her natural appearance. Use short-to-medium silver-blonde hair, realistic skin texture, fine lines, minimal makeup, a slate-blue blouse, a bright contemporary German bathroom and clean morning daylight. Landscape 4:3 over-shoulder composition with the reflection in sharp focus. The emotion is recognition and self-confidence, not vanity or a miraculous transformation. No text, logo, product branding, watermark, de-aging, glamour pose, clinic, before-after comparison or impossible mirror geometry.
